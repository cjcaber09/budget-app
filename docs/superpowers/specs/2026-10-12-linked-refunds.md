# Linked refunds

User approved refund-month accounting and refunds for ordinary expenses and paid bills. Full and multiple partial refunds are linked to an owned original expense. Refunded bills stay recorded/paid; refunds never settle, skip or regenerate bills. Income, opening balances, corrections and internal transfer principal are not refundable expenses. Transfer-fee expenses remain managed through their transfer.

Record refund lives in expense details. Amount defaults to the remaining refundable total; users choose a received date and receiving method, with an optional note. The original receipt/items/proofs remain unchanged. Refund history supports editing and confirmed removal. Main Transactions has a Refunds filter, and Expenses includes refunds so its history reconciles with net expense totals. Refund-row navigation opens its original expense with a fresh visit and the refund editor, retaining tab history Back. Help explains this entry point.

Refunds count on their received calendar date, never as earnings. Their category follows the original expense, while the credited method is independent. Current-month/category/account expenses can be negative after prior-month returns; budget remaining and net cash flow reflect that value. Reports decode signed expense sections only with refundVersion1. Negative categories disable misleading pie/percentage shares while retaining every signed amount. Trend charts plot both sides of zero; pace bars clamp visual widths without changing money. Bill refunds preserve bill provenance and do not distort discretionary forecasts or inflate legacy-duplicate counts. Native budget alert reached levels remain unchanged on refunds.

## Data and writes

Migration0034 introduces owner-RLS, read-only refunds and private creation receipts. Positive exact cent amounts have tenant parent/method FKs. Auth and original-expense cascades remove the new data during actual account deletion/reset; private account-operation jobs remain untouched. The original expense cannot be deleted, converted to income, reduced below its refunded total or moved after a refund date while linked refunds exist.

save_refund(jsonb) derives ownership, holds the existing financial lock and the parent row lock, enforces the remaining amount, and accepts only dates from the purchase through financial today for new/changed dates. Unchanged historical dates survive financial-timezone changes. Stable create UUID/canonical payload receipts return exact retries without overwriting subsequent edits; deleted refunds cannot be resurrected by replay. Versioned updates/deletes reject stale changes and recognize exact update retries. An expected expenseCreatedAt timestamp couples requests to the captured original instance, rejecting stale requests against a recreated UUID. A shared ID guard keeps transaction/refund activity IDs unique for each owner. Deferred constraints protect total caps even for privileged maintenance writes.

refund_snapshot(uuid) returns owned complete rows, original amount/creation timestamp and the exact refunded total. Client transport is decimal strings, aggregate transport exact integer-cent strings. Account balances add refunds using existing financial dates, assignment timestamps and baselines; credit refunds reduce debt and Cash initialization after historic refunds does not credit them twice. Refund-only activity requires confirmation before changing an opening balance. New choices exclude archived methods; unchanged assignments and exact retries remain valid.

Direct-column security-invoker financial_activity/financial_entries views preserve original table/item constraints. Activity rows use a distinct positive Refund type; aggregation rows subtract their amount from expenses. Existing snapshot, reporting, notification, exact export and bounded history RPC reads use the appropriate view without fetching rendered lists or independent labels. Interactive history keeps its10-row cursor/lookahead; complete report/export envelopes include more than1000 entries. CSV adds refund_of linkage and blank percentages when net distributions are not meaningful.

Failed loads/saves retain selections and drafts. Exact retry payloads retain their ID even after method archival or refreshed totals; arbitrary changes still receive server checks. RPC response ownership/identity is validated before closing. Error recovery is inline through existing mutation/query notification metadata. Refresh refunds and closing/reopening load changed versions. No ordinary save-transaction fallback can bypass the refund RPC. refundVersion capability hides unsupported filter/actions before deployment. The existing category-history read safely falls back to transactions only if the new read view is absent.
